# Project ARES-5

## Main Project Link

🎮 **[Play Project Ares 5 on itch.io](https://subha-jahan19.itch.io/project-ares-5)**

**An educational space exploration and survival game by Orbit Nexa**

Project ARES-5 is an interactive space exploration and survival game
designed to make space science more interesting, accessible, and easier
to understand through gameplay. Players take on the challenge of
planning and carrying out a mission from Earth to Mars, experiencing
some of the decisions and difficulties involved in space exploration.

The game is built around two stages: **Mission Planning** and **The
Journey**. Players must design a spacecraft within limited budget and
weight capacities, then manage a five-day journey by completing tasks,
maintaining essential resources, and responding to unexpected
challenges.

> **Project status:** Prototype. The current version is playable on
> mobile devices. A wider range of spacecraft options, missions,
> gameplay features, and a PC version are part of the team's future
> plans.

## Overview

Space missions are much more than the launch of a spacecraft. They
require careful planning, engineering, teamwork, resource management,
and the ability to respond to unexpected situations. Project ARES-5
turns these ideas into an interactive experience.

Players simulate a Mars mission themselves. Their decisions during
spacecraft preparation can affect the challenges they face later in the
journey. The game aims to help players understand the work behind space
exploration while developing an interest in science and technology.

## Gameplay

Project ARES-5 is divided into two main chapters.

### Chapter 1: The Mission

Players design their spacecraft by selecting components and essential
supplies, including the spacecraft structure and food supply.

Each available component has its own **cost** and **mass**. Players have
a limited budget and carrying capacity, so they cannot simply select
every premium option. They must balance their choices and consider how
each decision may affect the journey.

This stage introduces the importance of:

-   Spacecraft design and mission preparation
-   Budget and mass management
-   Evaluating the advantages and disadvantages of equipment
-   Making strategic decisions with limited resources

### Chapter 2: The Journey

After completing the planning stage, players begin a **five-day space
journey**.

During the journey, players must:

-   Complete daily mission tasks.
-   Monitor their health and essential resources.
-   Respond to unexpected emergencies.
-   Deal with external challenges.
-   Make timely decisions when situations change.

The journey demonstrates that even a carefully planned mission can
encounter unexpected problems. Players must think critically, stay
focused, and manage their resources to keep the mission on track.

### Mission Restart System

Project ARES-5 includes a restart system. If a player makes a critical
mistake or fails to complete an important mission objective, the mission
fails and the game restarts from the beginning.

This system reinforces the importance of preparation, careful resource
management, and decision-making throughout the mission.

## Key Features

-   **Spacecraft customization:** Select spacecraft components and
    essential supplies.
-   **Budget and mass limits:** Make trade-offs while working within
    limited resources.
-   **Five-day mission:** Experience a multi-day space journey.
-   **Resource management:** Keep track of health and essential
    spacecraft resources.
-   **Daily tasks:** Complete objectives throughout the journey.
-   **Emergencies and external challenges:** React to unexpected
    situations.
-   **Mission restart system:** Start over after a critical mission
    failure.
-   **Educational gameplay:** Explore ideas related to space missions
    through interactive decisions.

## Main Objectives

Project ARES-5 aims to:

1.  Make space exploration more interesting and enjoyable, especially
    for students.
2.  Demonstrate the importance of mission planning and spacecraft
    design.
3.  Teach effective budget, mass, and resource management.
4.  Help players understand challenges astronauts may face during space
    travel.
5.  Develop problem-solving, critical-thinking, and scientific-thinking
    skills.
6.  Show how preparation and quick decisions contribute to a successful
    mission.
7.  Encourage curiosity about science, technology, and space
    exploration.

## Our Vision

Our goal is not simply to create a game. We want to build an experience
where people can learn about space while enjoying an exciting adventure.

Project ARES-5 gives players the opportunity to experience the planning
and challenges of a Mars mission from home. It aims to make complex
space-related ideas easier to explore while encouraging creativity,
curiosity, critical thinking, teamwork, and problem-solving.

We hope the project will inspire students and space enthusiasts to
appreciate the work scientists and engineers put into space
missions---and encourage the next generation to explore new
possibilities.

## About Orbit Nexa

Orbit Nexa is a team of young individuals working to make space and
astronomy more understandable and engaging.

The team's wider goal is to take complex topics in astronomy and turn
them into experiences that people can learn from and enjoy. Project
ARES-5 is one step toward that goal.
